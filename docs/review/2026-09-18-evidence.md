## Pricing and comparison rules

Campaign freshness and benefit freshness are different. A current coupon must not make a policy look verified. Campaign windows use Hong Kong calendar days and expire at the next Hong Kong midnight, including when a tab is kept open or brought back into view. Reviews older than 31 days do not keep campaigns active. Future, impossible and malformed dates stay unknown.

A minimum pulled from prose is not a comparable premium: monthly vs yearly, individual vs family, annual vs single-trip, age, occupation, room class and deductible all change the basis. The price ruler, budget bands, price sort and fabricated discount amounts have been removed from active comparison surfaces. Original premium text remains in labelled snapshot disclosures where available.

## Remaining release risk

Many records have weak, reused or unrelated quotations; product-family identity and current contract versions remain the largest unresolved risk. Keep explicit uncertainty, original-source access and the data-quality route. Do not advertise this PR as “all insurance verified”. Next semantic reviews should start with mismatched contract titles, 404 product pages and repeated quotations, then match certification/plan IDs and benefit units one row at a time.
